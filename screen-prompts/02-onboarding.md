# Screen 02 — Onboarding

## Goal
Explain the app’s core value in a short, welcoming introduction and let users reach the app quickly.

## Detailed generation prompt
Create a premium, friendly onboarding screen for “Vivah Bio”, a marriage biodata maker for Indian users. Use the shared ivory, deep-plum, muted-gold palette and rounded modern components. The hero illustration should show a beautifully formatted biodata page with a small portrait placeholder, elegant section dividers, and two or three tasteful decorative motifs. Do not use a real person’s photo. Headline: “Create a biodata that feels like you”. Supporting copy: “Choose a design, add your details, and export a polished biodata to share with family.” Include three small pagination dots, a prominent “Get Started” button, and a low-emphasis “Skip” text action. Keep the content focused and avoid multiple paragraphs.

## UI elements
- Top-right “Skip” action.
- Central illustration with clear hierarchy.
- Headline and concise description.
- Page indicator dots (current page clearly indicated).
- Primary “Get Started” button anchored above the bottom safe area.

## Functionality
- Support a short 2–3 page onboarding sequence if the prototype includes swiping; each page should have a distinct benefit (beautiful templates, easy guided details, export/share).
- “Get Started” and “Skip” both enter the home dashboard and mark onboarding as completed.
- Swiping and tapping pagination dots must remain synchronized.
- Returning users should not be forced through onboarding again.
- Do not ask for account creation or permissions during onboarding.

## Accessibility and layout
Maintain readable contrast, large tap targets, and enough spacing between Skip and other controls. Support small screens without clipping the main CTA. Use illustrations rather than screenshots or photos.

## Output requested
Generate only the onboarding screen, with a clear visual hierarchy and the interactions above represented in the design.
